1978. Employees Whose Manager Left the Company

Table: Employees
Column NameType
employee_idint
namevarchar
manager_idint
salaryint
employee_id is the primary key.
A manager is considered to have "left" if their manager_id is listed for an employee, but their own employee_id no longer exists in the table.
Problem Statement
Find the IDs of employees who satisfy both conditions:
  • Salary is strictly less than $30,000.
  • Their manager left the company (manager_id is not in the employee_id column).
Return the result table ordered by employee_id.
Example 1 Input
employee_idnamemanager_idsalary
3Mila960301
12Antonellanull31000
1Kalel1121241
9Mikaelanull50937
11Joziah628485
Output:
employee_id
11
Explanation:
- Employees with salary < 30k: Kalel (1) and Joziah (11).
- Kalel's manager is 11. Joziah (11) is still in the table.
- Joziah's manager is 6. Employee 6 is not in the table.
- Result: 11.
