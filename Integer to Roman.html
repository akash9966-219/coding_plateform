12. Integer to Roman

Convert an integer between 1 and 3999 into a Roman numeral.

Roman numerals are formed by appending conversions of decimal place values from highest to lowest. Special "subtractive forms" are used for 4 and 9 at each decimal level (IV, IX, XL, XC, CD, CM).
Example 1:
Input: num = 3749
Output: "MMMDCCXLIX"
Explanation: 3000(MMM) + 700(DCC) + 40(XL) + 9(IX)
Example 3:
Input: num = 1994
Output: "MCMXCIV"
Explanation: 1000(M) + 900(CM) + 90(XC) + 4(IV)
Constraints:
  • 1 <= num <= 3999
