238. Product Except Self

Return an array where each element is the product of all elements except itself. Division is not allowed.
Logic:
1. Prefix Pass: res[i] = product of everything to the left of i.
2. Suffix Pass: Multiply res[i] by the rolling product of everything to the right.
3. Space: Output array is O(1) per the follow-up, total auxiliary space is O(1).
Complexity:
  • Time: O(n) - Two linear passes.
  • Space: O(1) - Constant auxiliary space (excluding output).
