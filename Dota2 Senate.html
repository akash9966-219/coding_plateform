649. Dota2 Senate

Two parties: Radiant and Dire. Senators take turns to ban an opponent. If a party has no senators left, the other party wins.
Examples:
Example 1:
Input: senate = "RD"
Output: "Radiant"
Explanation: R bans D. Only R remains.
Logic:
1. Two Queues: Store indices of 'R' in one queue and 'D' in another.
2. Turn: Compare front indices. The smaller index bans the larger one.
3. Next Round: The winner is pushed back to their queue with index + n.
4. Termination: Repeat until one queue is empty.
Complexity:
  • Time: O(n) - Every senator is processed and potentially banned once.
  • Space: O(n) - Storing indices in queues.
