188. Buy/Sell Stock IV

Calculate maximum profit given at most k transactions. You must sell before you can buy again.
Generalized Logic:

K-state tracking (1 to k)

For each transaction i in 1..k:
1. buy[i] = min(buy[i], price - profit[i-1])
2. profit[i] = max(profit[i], price - buy[i])

Final Answer: profit[k]
Complexity:
  • Time: O(n * k)
  • Space: O(k)
