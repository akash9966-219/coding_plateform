148. Sort List

Sort a linked list in O(n log n) time and O(1) auxiliary space (ignoring recursion stack).
Logic:
1. Find middle with slow/fast pointers.
2. Split list into two halves.
3. Recurse and merge.
Constraints:
  • Number of nodes up to 5 * 104
  • Values up to 105
