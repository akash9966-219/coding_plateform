918. Maximum Sum Circular Subarray

Given a circular array, find the maximum possible sum of a non-empty subarray.
Visual Logic: The Two Scenarios
Scenario 1: Standard Subarray

The maximum sum is in the middle of the array (Standard Kadane's).

Scenario 2: Circular (Wrap-Around)

Maximum sum wraps around. This equals Total Sum - Minimum Subarray Sum.
