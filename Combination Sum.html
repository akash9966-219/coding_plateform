39. Combination Sum

Find all unique combinations in candidates where the numbers sum to target. You may reuse candidates indefinitely.
Example 1:
Input: candidates = [2,3,6,7], target = 7
Output: [[2,2,3],[7]]
Constraints:
  • 1 ≤ candidates.length ≤ 30
  • Same number can be used unlimited times.
  • All candidates are distinct.
