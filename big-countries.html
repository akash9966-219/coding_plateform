595. Big Countries

Table: World
Column NameType
namevarchar
continentvarchar
areaint
populationint
gdpbigint
name is the primary key (column with unique values) for this table.
Each row of this table gives information about the name of a country, the continent to which it belongs, its area, the population, and its GDP value.
Definition of a Big Country
A country is big if:
- It has an area of at least three million (3,000,000 km²), or
- It has a population of at least twenty-five million (25,000,000).
Problem Statement
Write a solution to find the name, population, and area of the big countries. Return the result table in any order.
