295. Data Stream Median

Find the median of a stream of integers. Maintain two halves of the data using a Max-Heap (lower half) and a Min-Heap (upper half).
Logic:
1. Small Half: Max-Heap stores numbers $\le$ median.
2. Large Half: Min-Heap stores numbers $\ge$ median.
3. Rebalance: Ensure sizes differ by at most 1.
addNum: $O(\log n)$ | findMedian: $O(1)$
Complexity:
  • Time: O(log n) per insertion.
  • Space: O(n) to store numbers.
