560. Subarray Sum K

Find the total number of contiguous subarrays whose sum equals k.
Logic:
1. Prefix Sums: Calculate the cumulative sum as you traverse.
2. The Gap: If currentSum - k was seen before, a subarray exists.
3. Frequency Map: Store how many times each prefix sum has appeared.
O(n) Time | O(n) Space.
Complexity:
  • Time: O(n)
  • Space: O(n)
