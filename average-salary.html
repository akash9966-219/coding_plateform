1491. Average Salary

Description

You are given an array of unique integers salary where salary[i] is the salary of the ith employee.

Return the average salary of the employees excluding the minimum and maximum salary. Answers within 10-5 of the actual answer will be accepted.

Example 1
Input salary = [4000, 3000, 1000, 2000]
Output 2500.00000
Explanation Minimum salary is 1000 and maximum salary is 4000.
Average salary excluding minimum and maximum is (2000+3000) / 2 = 2500.
