433. Minimum Genetic Mutation

Given a startGene and endGene, find the minimum number of mutations to transform one into the other. Each mutation must exist in the bank.
Example 2:
Input: startGene = "AACCGGTT", endGene = "AAACGGTA"
Bank: ["AACCGGTA", "AACCGCTA", "AAACGGTA"]
Output: 2
Constraints:
  • Gene length is exactly 8.
  • Characters are only 'A', 'C', 'G', 'T'.
  • Bank length is up to 10.
