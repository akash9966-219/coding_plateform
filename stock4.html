188. Stock IV (k Transactions)

Description

You are given an integer k and an array prices.

Find the maximum profit you can achieve. You may complete at most k transactions.

Example
Input k = 2, prices = [3,2,6,5,0,3]
Output 7
Explanation Buy at price 2, sell at 6 (profit 4). Then buy at 0, sell at 3 (profit 3). Total = 7.
