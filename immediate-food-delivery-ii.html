1174. Immediate Food Delivery II

Table: Delivery
Column NameType
delivery_idint
customer_idint
order_datedate
customer_pref_delivery_datedate
delivery_id is the primary key.
Immediate: Preferred Date == Order Date.
Scheduled: Preferred Date != Order Date.
First Order: Earliest order date for a customer.
Problem Statement
Find the percentage of immediate orders in the first orders of all customers, rounded to 2 decimal places.
Example 1 Input
customer_idorder_datepref_date
12019-08-012019-08-02
22019-08-022019-08-02
32019-08-212019-08-22
42019-08-092019-08-09
Output:
immediate_percentage
50.00
Explanation:
- Cust 1 First Order: Scheduled.
- Cust 2 First Order: Immediate.
- Cust 3 First Order: Scheduled.
- Cust 4 First Order: Immediate.
Total First Orders = 4. Immediate = 2. Percentage = 50.00%
