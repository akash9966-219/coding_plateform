2542. Maximum Subsequence Score

Choose a subsequence of length k. Maximize: (sum of nums1 elements) * (min of nums2 elements).
Sample Test Case:
nums1nums2kMax Score
[1,3,3,2][2,1,3,4]312
Greedy Strategy:
1. Sort pairs by nums2 descending.
2. Maintain Min-Heap of k largest nums1 values.
3. Score = (Current Heap Sum) * (Current nums2 value).
Complexity:
  • Time: O(N log N)
  • Space: O(N)
