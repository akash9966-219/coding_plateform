682. Baseball Game

Description

You are keeping the scores for a baseball game with strange rules. The game consists of several rounds, where the scores of past rounds may affect future rounds' scores.

You are given a list of strings ops, where ops[i] is the ith operation you must apply to the record:

  • An integer x: Record a new score of x.
  • "+": Record a new score that is the sum of the previous two scores.
  • "D": Record a new score that is double the previous score.
  • "C": Invalidate the previous score, removing it from the record.
Return the sum of all the scores on the record.

Example 1
Input ops = ["5","2","C","D","+"]
Output 30
Explanation "5": [5]
"2": [5, 2]
"C": [5]
"D": [5, 10]
"+": [5, 10, 15]
Sum: 5 + 10 + 15 = 30
