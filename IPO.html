502. IPO

Maximize your final capital by choosing at most k projects. You need capital[i] to start a project and receive profit[i] upon completion.
Greedy Execution:

Max-Heap tracks "Affordable Profits"

Current Capital: w
1. Projects with Capital ≤ w → Max-Heap
2. w += Max-Heap.pop()
3. Repeat k times or until Heap empty.
Complexity:
  • Time: O(N log N + K log N)
  • Space: O(N)
