180. Consecutive Numbers

Table: Logs
Column NameType
idint
numvarchar
id is the primary key and autoincrements.
Find numbers appearing at least three times consecutively.
Example 1 Input
idnum
11
21
31
42
51
Output:
ConsecutiveNums
1
Explanation:
1 appears at IDs 1, 2, and 3. This is a consecutive sequence of 3.
