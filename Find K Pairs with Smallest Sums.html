373. K Smallest Sum Pairs

Find k pairs with the smallest sums from two non-decreasing sorted arrays.
Binary Search/Heap Cross-Logic:

Exploration Frontier

(0,0) → (0,1) or (1,0)
(1,0) → (1,1) or (2,0)
Keep tracking the boundary in a Min-Heap.
Complexity:
  • Time: O(k log k)
  • Space: O(k)
