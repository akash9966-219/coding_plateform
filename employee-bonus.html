577. Employee Bonus

Table: Employee
Column NameType
empIdint
namevarchar
supervisorint
salaryint
Table: Bonus
Column NameType
empIdint
bonusint
empId is the primary key for both tables.
Bonus table empId is a foreign key to Employee table.
Problem Statement
Report the name and bonus amount of each employee who satisfies either:
- The employee has a bonus less than 1000.
- The employee did not get any bonus (null).
Example 1:

Input: Employee Table

empIdnamesalary
3Brad4000
1John1000
2Dan2000
4Thomas4000

Input: Bonus Table

empIdbonus
2500
42000

Output:

namebonus
Bradnull
Johnnull
Dan500
Explanation:
Brad and John have no entries in Bonus (null). Dan has 500 (< 1000). Thomas has 2000, so he is excluded.
