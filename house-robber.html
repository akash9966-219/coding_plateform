198. House Robber

Find the maximum amount of money you can rob tonight without robbing two adjacent houses.
Examples:
Input: nums = [1,2,3,1]
Output: 4 (House 1 + House 3)
Input: nums = [2,7,9,3,1]
Output: 12 (House 1 + House 3 + House 5)
DP Logic:
For each house n:
1. temp: Store the current max.
2. robNext: max(robPrevious + n, robNext).
3. robPrevious: Update with temp.
Basically: f(i) = max(f(i-1), f(i-2) + nums[i])
Complexity:
  • Time: O(n) - Single pass.
  • Space: O(1) - Only two variables.
