70. Climbing Stairs

It takes n steps to reach the top. Each time you can climb 1 or 2 steps. How many distinct ways can you reach the top?
Dynamic Programming Logic:

Ways(n) = Ways(n-1) + Ways(n-2)

Step 1: 1 way
Step 2: 2 ways
Step 3: 1 + 2 = 3 ways
Step 4: 2 + 3 = 5 ways
Complexity:
  • Time: O(n)
  • Space: O(1)
