1321. Restaurant Growth

Table: Customer
Column NameType
customer_idint
namevarchar
visited_ondate
amountint
In SQL, (customer_id, visited_on) is the primary key.
This table contains transaction data. At least one customer visits every day.
Problem Statement
You are the restaurant owner analyzing a possible expansion. Compute the moving average of customer payments in a seven days window (i.e., current day + 6 days before).

- average_amount must be rounded to two decimal places.
- Return results ordered by visited_on ascending.
Example 1 Input
Customer entries spanning from 2019-01-01 to 2019-01-10. Note that on 01-10, multiple customers (Jhon and Jade) visited.
Output:
visited_onamountaverage_amount
2019-01-07860122.86
2019-01-08840120.00
2019-01-09840120.00
2019-01-101000142.86
Step-by-Step Explanation:
1st Window (01-01 to 01-07): Sum = 860, Avg = 860/7 = 122.86
2nd Window (01-02 to 01-08): Sum = 840, Avg = 840/7 = 120.00
...
4th Window (01-04 to 01-10): Sum (includes both Jhon & Jade on the 10th) = 1000, Avg = 142.86
