(player_id, event_date) is the primary key.
A retention occurs if a player logs in on first_login_date + 1.
Problem Statement
Report the fraction of players that logged in again on the day after the day they first logged in, rounded to 2 decimal places.
Example 1 Input
player_idevent_date
12016-03-01
12016-03-02
22017-06-25
32016-03-02
32018-07-03
Output:
fraction
0.33
Explanation:
Total Unique Players = 3 (IDs 1, 2, 3).
Player 1 first login: 03-01. Logged in 03-02 (Success).
Player 2 first login: 06-25. No login 06-26 (Fail).
Player 3 first login: 03-02. No login 03-03 (Fail).
Result: 1 / 3 = 0.33
