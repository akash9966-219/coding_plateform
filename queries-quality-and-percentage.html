1211. Queries Quality and Percentage

Table: Queries
Column NameType
query_namevarchar
resultvarchar
positionint
ratingint
The position column has values 1 to 500.
A poor query is defined as one with a rating less than 3.
Problem Statement
Find each query_name, its quality, and its poor_query_percentage.
- Quality: Average of (rating / position).
- Poor Percentage: Percentage of queries with rating < 3.
Round both to 2 decimal places.
Example 1 Input
query_nameresultpositionrating
DogGolden Retriever15
DogGerman Shepherd25
DogMule2001
CatShirazi52
Output:
query_namequalitypoor_query_percentage
Dog2.5033.33
Cat0.6633.33
Explanation:
Dog Quality: ((5/1) + (5/2) + (1/200)) / 3 = 2.50
Dog Poor %: (1 poor query / 3 total) * 100 = 33.33%
