1164. Product Price at a Given Date

Table: Products
Column NameType
product_idint
new_priceint
change_datedate
(product_id, change_date) is the primary key.
Initially, all products have price 10.
Problem Statement
Find the prices of all products on the date 2019-08-16.
Example 1 Input
product_idnew_pricechange_date
1202019-08-14
1352019-08-16
2652019-08-17
3202019-08-18
Output:
product_idprice
135
210
310
Explanation:
Product 1: Latest change before 08-16 was on 08-16 (Price 35).
Product 2: First change is 08-17 (after deadline), so price is default 10.
Product 3: First change is 08-18 (after deadline), so price is default 10.
