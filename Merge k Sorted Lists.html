23. Merge k Sorted Lists

Given k sorted linked lists, merge them into one single sorted linked list.
Merge Strategy Visualization:
Complexity:
Time: O(N log k), where N is the total number of nodes and k is the number of lists.
Space: O(k) for the priority queue or O(1) for iterative divide and conquer.
Constraints:
  • k up to 104
  • Total nodes up to 104
  • Each individual list is pre-sorted.
