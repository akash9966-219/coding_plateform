11. Container With Water

Find two lines that form a container that holds the most water.
Example 1:

Input: height = [1,8,6,2,5,4,8,3,7]
Output: 49
Logic:
1. Two Pointers: Start with left = 0 and right = n-1.
2. Area: min(h[l], h[r]) * (r - l).
3. Shrink: Move the pointer pointing to the shorter line.
4. Why? Moving the taller line can only decrease the area (width decreases, height is still limited by the original short line).
Complexity:
  • Time: O(n) - Single pass through the array.
  • Space: O(1) - Constant space approach.
