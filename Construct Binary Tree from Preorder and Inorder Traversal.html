105. Tree Reconstruction

Construct a binary tree from its preorder and inorder traversal arrays.

Note: The arrays consist of unique values and are guaranteed to be valid traversals.
Example 1:
Input: preorder = [3,9,20,15,7], inorder = [9,3,15,20,7]
Output: [3,9,20,null,null,15,7]
Constraints:
  • 1 <= preorder.length <= 3000
  • Traversals consist of unique values.
