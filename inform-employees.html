1376. Inform All Employees

Description

A company has n employees with IDs 0 to n - 1. The head has headID. Information spreads from managers to subordinates.

Employee i takes informTime[i] minutes to inform all their direct subordinates. Return the total time needed to inform all employees.

Example
Input n = 6, headID = 2, manager = [2,2,-1,2,2,2], informTime = [0,0,1,0,0,0]
Output 1
Explanation CEO (ID 2) takes 1 min to inform everyone else simultaneously.
