1341. Movie Rating

Table: MovieRating
Column NameType
movie_idint
user_idint
ratingint
created_atdate
(movie_id, user_id) is the primary key.
Each row contains the rating of a movie by a user and the date of review.
Problem Statement
1. Find the user name who has rated the greatest number of movies. (Tie-break: lexicographical order).
2. Find the movie title with the highest average rating in February 2020. (Tie-break: lexicographical order).
Example 1 Explanation
- Daniel and Monica rated 3 movies each. Daniel is lexicographically smaller.
- "Frozen 2" and "Joker" average 3.5 in Feb. "Frozen 2" is lexicographically smaller.
Output:
results
Daniel
Frozen 2
