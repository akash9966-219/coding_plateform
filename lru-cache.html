146. LRU Cache

Design a Least Recently Used (LRU) cache. Support get and put in O(1) average time.
Logic:
1. HashMap: Provides $O(1)$ access to nodes.
2. Doubly Linked List: Maintains usage order.
3. MRU (Head): Most recently used items moved here.
4. LRU (Tail): Items here are evicted first.
Complexity:
  • Time: O(1) for both operations.
  • Space: O(capacity).
