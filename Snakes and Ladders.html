909. Snakes and Ladders

Find the minimum dice rolls to reach the end of a Boustrophedon grid. This is a Shortest Path problem on an unweighted graph, perfectly suited for BFS.
Constraints:
  • n is between 2 and 20.
  • Roll a 6-sided die: [curr+1, min(curr+6, n²)].
