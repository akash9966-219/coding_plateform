84. Largest Rectangle

Find the area of the largest rectangle in a histogram. Solve in O(n) time using a monotonic stack.
Example 1:

Input: heights = [2,1,5,6,2,3]
Output: 10
Logic:
1. Stack: Maintain an increasing stack of indices.
2. Shrink: When a shorter bar appears, pop and calculate area for the taller bar.
3. Boundaries: The current index is the right boundary; the new top of stack is the left boundary.
4. Sentinel: Add a zero height at the end to flush the stack.
Complexity:
  • Time: O(n) - Each bar is pushed and popped once.
  • Space: O(n) - Stack stores indices.
