585. Investments in 2016

Table: Insurance
Column NameType
pidint
tiv_2015float
tiv_2016float
latfloat
lonfloat
pid is the primary key. tiv_2015 is total investment value in 2015, tiv_2016 in 2016. lat and lon are city coordinates.
Problem Statement
Write a solution to report the sum of all tiv_2016 values for all policyholders who:
  • Have the same tiv_2015 value as one or more other policyholders.
  • Are not located in the same city as any other policyholder (the (lat, lon) attribute pairs must be unique).
Round tiv_2016 to two decimal places.
Example 1 Explanation
- The first record (PID 1) and last record (PID 4) meet both criteria: tiv_2015 of 10 is shared, but their locations (10,10) and (40,40) are unique.
- PID 2 fails because its tiv_2015 is unique and its location is shared with PID 3.
- PID 3 fails because its location is shared with PID 2.
Output:
tiv_2016
45.00
