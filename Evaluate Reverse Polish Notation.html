150. Evaluate RPN

Evaluate an arithmetic expression in Reverse Polish Notation. Valid operators are +, -, *, and /.

Division between two integers always truncates toward zero.
Example 1:
Input: tokens = ["2","1","+","3","*"]
Output: 9
Explanation: ((2 + 1) * 3) = 9
Constraints:
  • 1 <= tokens.length <= 104
  • tokens[i] is an operator or integer in range [-200, 200].
