121. Buy & Sell Stock

Find the maximum profit you can achieve by choosing a single day to buy one stock and a different day in the future to sell it.
Examples:
Input: prices = [7,1,5,3,6,4]
Output: 5 (Buy at 1, Sell at 6)
Input: prices = [7,6,4,3,1]
Output: 0
Greedy Logic:
1. minPrice: Keep track of the lowest price found so far.
2. currentProfit: Current price minus minPrice.
3. maxProfit: Update if currentProfit is higher.
Single Pass $O(n)$ Solution.
Complexity:
  • Time: O(n) - One pass through the array.
  • Space: O(1) - Only two tracking variables.
