610. Triangle Judgement

Table: Triangle
Column NameType
xint
yint
zint
(x, y, z) is the primary key.
Each row contains the lengths of three line segments.
Problem Statement
Report for every three line segments whether they can form a triangle.
Example 1 Input
xyz
131530
102015
Output:
xyztriangle
131530No
102015Yes
Geometric Logic:
A triangle is valid if:
- x + y > z
- x + z > y
- y + z > x
